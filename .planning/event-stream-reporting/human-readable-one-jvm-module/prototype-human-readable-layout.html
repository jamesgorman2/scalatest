How does the human-readable output look?

Each scenario is the consolidated tree a set of fixed event streams gives for one test command (shown under Input). Every scope and test carries its name and its label, the text ScalaTest's formatter gives it in that suite style (control words such as should, when, Scenario:). Labels, failure messages and stack traces are taken from a real ScalaTest 3.2.19 run.

Indentation is one rule: every line sits 2 spaces in from the line it belongs to. A label's suites sit under it, a suite's scopes, tests and messages under the suite, a scope's children under the scope, and a test's failure, messages and stack trace under the test.
